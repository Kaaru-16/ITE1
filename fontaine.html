A Glimpse of Your Journey

DAY 01

Arrival at the Aquatic Capital

Settle into your luxurious accommodations within the pristine Court of Fontaine. Spend your first day and night marveling at the towering architecture and enjoying the refined culinary scene of the high-tier districts.

DAY 02

Waters of Remembrance

Take a relaxing ride on the Navia Line toward Erinnyes and spend a peaceful afternoon at the Lucine Fountain.

DAY 03

The Grand Spectacle

Step into the magnificent Opera Epiclese to witness Fontaine's world-renowned blend of breathtaking theatrical performance and dramatic judicial proceedings.

DAY 04

Elegance and the Abyss

Enjoy a free day to dive into the crystal-clear waters of the Great Fontaine Lake or browse the exquisite fashion boutiques of the Quartier Lyonnais.

DAY 05

A Stylish Departure

Savor a fresh glass of Fonta and take one last leisurely morning stroll along the majestic aqueducts before gathering your belongings and bidding adieu to the Nation of Justice.
